About Us

The Amravati Zilla Parishad Shikshak Sahakari Bank Ltd., Amravati is a trusted financial institution serving the people of Amravati district. With its head office located in Amravati, the bank plays a key role in providing reliable and transparent cooperative banking services.

The Amravati Zilla Parishad Shikshak Sahakari Bank Ltd., Amravati offers a wide range of banking facilities including deposits, loans, and secure digital services through its user-friendly mobile application. Customers can easily check balances, transfer funds, and access account information anytime, anywhere.

The bank is enabled for modern payment systems such as NEFT and RTGS, making transactions smooth and convenient for individuals as well as businesses.

By combining cooperative values with modern technology, The Amravati Zilla Parishad Shikshak Sahakari Bank Ltd., The Amravati Zilla Parishad Shikshak Sahakari Bank Ltd., Amravati continues to support the financial growth and security of its members and the wider community across Amravati district.
