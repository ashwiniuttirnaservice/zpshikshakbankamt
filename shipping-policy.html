Service & Delivery Policy

1. Introduction

At The Amravati Zilla Parishad Shikshak Sahakari Bank Ltd., Amravati, we are committed to delivering reliable and efficient banking services to all our customers. This policy outlines how we provide services across our branches and digital platforms while maintaining transparency and customer satisfaction.

2. Transaction Processing

All banking transactions including deposits, withdrawals, fund transfers, and loan disbursements are processed promptly and securely. Our goal is to ensure minimal delays and smooth service delivery both at branches and through online channels.

3. Availability of Services

Banking facilities are available during official working hours at our branches and 24/7 through our mobile banking and digital platforms. We continuously strive to provide uninterrupted access to essential banking services.

4. Account & Service Registration

Customers can open accounts, apply for loans, and register for digital banking services with ease. Once registered, they can enjoy seamless access to a variety of cooperative banking facilities.

5. Customer Support

Our dedicated support team is available to assist with queries related to accounts, transactions, loans, or digital banking services. Customers are encouraged to contact us through our branch network, helplines, or official communication channels.

6. Regular Updates

We regularly update our services, technology, and policies to align with regulatory requirements and modern banking practices. Customers are kept informed about important updates through official notices, our website, and mobile app.

Thank you for banking with zpshikshakbankamt. If you have any questions or require further assistance, please reach out to our customer support team. We look forward to serving your financial needs with trust and dedication.
